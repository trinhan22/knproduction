Get To Know Us

1

Do you work exclusively on media & production projects?

KN Production focuses on creating impactful visual stories through media and production. Every project or client we take on is an opportunity to transform ideas into compelling visual experiences — from concept and photography to filmmaking and creative production.

2

Are there organisations you won't work with?

We carefully select the projects and clients we work with. We believe in working with brands, organisations and individuals who value creativity, authenticity and meaningful storytelling. We aim to create work that is purposeful, professional and true to the story behind every project.

3

A Creative Studio? Can you explain?

We are more than just a production company - KN Production brings ideas to life from concept to final delivery. From creative direction and photography to filmmaking, post-production and visual content, we provide end-to-end production solutions tailored to each project, while keeping every detail consistent with the story and vision.

4

How can I work with KN Production /
how do I get involved?

We’d love to hear from you! Whether you have a project in mind, need creative production support, or simply want to explore an idea, KN Production is always open to new collaborations. Get in touch with us and let’s turn your ideas into stories worth telling. !

5

Can I screen your films?

For questions about distribution, email us at knproduction.work@gmail.com

6

Are you hiring?

At the moment we are not hiring or accepting interns, but we’re always interested to meet new artists. Don’t hesitate to send your introduction to: knproduction.work@gmail.com .
